Hot Cross Buns 2026

I was on a sourdough kick for a while, and here are the results from trying to make some sourdough hot cross buns, with varying success and varying documentation

Farm House Kitchen

https://farmhousekitchen.co.nz/recipe/sourdough-hot-cross-buns/

“On Good Friday traditionally we planted Spring bulbs and baked these hot cross buns to have with our dinner in the evening, like a rich vegetable soup. It was a great event with the children mixing and shaping the buns, and then decorating with the crosses to remember Christ’s death on the Cross.”

  1. Hot cross buns should not be eaten like a dinner roll with soup.
  2. I understand it’s in the name but I’m not interested the redemption of the saviour.
  3. However, the recipe looks good. It uses a combination of plain and wholemeal flour, which is good. Chopped apple is a good addition.
  4. Measuring sourdough starter in cups and not grams is different to every other recipe I’ve seen anywhere
  5. Made these ones first (7-8 March)

Result

These came out really well! It took longer than expected for the rising to happen. I have also learnt that Instagram doesn’t save stories by default so I’ve only got a few pictures. Good flavour, the jam glaze was maybe a bit much, nice dense texture, big buns. They were still good a couple of days later in the sandwich press. 10/10

Pantry Mama

https://pantrymama.com/sourdough-hot-cross-buns/

Rating: 4.32 from 44 reviews

“These could make 12 large or 18 smaller buns.” Ha! I don’t want smaller buns.

The photos don’t look like they have much fruit in them, and the buns are very pale. There aren’t many other photos but I’ll be ok, I hope.

Cinnamon and butter together is a good idea. One egg plus one egg yolk is not a good idea. Adding the fruit to hot water is a good idea, rather than just a little vanilla or juice like a couple of the other recipes. Let’s give it a go (14-15 March)

Result

These were fine, might be the softest sourdough I’ve made, but weren’t that great as hot cross buns.

Amy Bakes Bread

https://amybakesbread.com/sourdough-hot-cross-buns/

Rating: 5 from 17 reviews

I don’t like the “why you’ll love these” section with links to other recipes. “Important ingredients” is all of the ingredients. The first picture is not promising, there’s no distinction between top and bottom, and the glaze looks like a donut. I appreciate the timeline, and the two different recipes for cinnamon rolls.

Let’s give it a go (21-22 March)

Result

These were fine. They took a long time to rise and I probably got impatient and didn’t roll them properly and maybe I need to wait some more for the starter as well but they were nice enough. 10/10 for effort

The Clever Carrot

https://www.theclevercarrot.com/2020/03/sourdough-hot-cross-buns/

Rating: 4.9 from 93 reviews

The secret to these (apparently) is to combine the flour and butter first. This is like making scones, which sounds like it could be a good idea. Cream cheese icing for the crosses a bit strange. Pictures look good, the buns might be a little small though. The bulk rise for the dough should triple in size! That sounds like a lot. I don’t have pumpkin pie spice, but otherwise the recipe seems achievable.

Let’s do this (3-4 April)

Result

Round buns!

These look great, but cream cheese crosses is not the one, soaking fruit in vanilla essence is not it, and they’re almost soft but not quite.

That Sourdough Gal

https://thatsourdoughgal.com/the-best-sourdough-hot-cross-buns-recipe-so-soft/

Rating: 5 from 10 reviews

Do I trust folks that haven’t eaten a hot cross bun before? Sweet stiff starter, and the Tangzhong method? Huh? This isn’t a great start. Tested by 18 readers around the world! Having this kind of clout is pretty cool. I think most of these recipes are going to be made over a weekend, because there are quite a few steps and they will be dependent on how the dough is rising. Detailed instructions and pictures are helpful. “If your dough rises at 21°C then it might take 7 business days.” This joke is not helpful! Overall this is a really long post which may or may not be helpful. I’m doubtful of the egg wash.
