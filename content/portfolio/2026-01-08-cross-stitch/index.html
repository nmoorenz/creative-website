Cross stitching two Japanese figures.

I bought these in 2014. They took some time to stitch, because there is a lot of stitching and because I procrastinated. It took a couple of years to do the man and one year to do the woman. There was a bit more motivation to get it finished after seeing the full results. Four years after finishing them my mum got them framed by a friend, and the framer said they were the best work she’s ever seen.

I’m quite proud of them.
