McDonalds

McDonald’s Hot Cross Bun Pie, $4 Half custard, half spiced fruit, which was mainly sultanas. Respectable effort from the golden arches. Same pastry as apple pies. 6/10 room to move on my rankings. Had to explain to workmates that I post about hot cross buns and mince pies every year. “Oh what about…” and “Are you going to try…” Watch this space! This comment thread, more exactly.

Filling was split lengthways, good for production lines. Crispy and tasty. Meets expectations.

I’m absolutely here for the production line commentary

Decent advertising

PaknSave

Paknsave 6 for $5.99

Bought these on the way up to Jaggers Camp in Whangarei Heads for a 40th birthday. Breakfast of champions and apparently I was a little hungover hungry so had 4.

Quite yum, better with a little olivani when I got home. Currants and sultanas.

Daily Bread

Daily Bread, Chocolate and Sour Cherry, $24 for 6

These are consistently good, the best in NZ for three years in a row, they prominently tell me. I got one regular and 6 of the chocolate. The cherry doesn’t come through strongly but it’s there. The chocolate is in chunks and very yum. Dense and delicious.

9/10 gimme some more cherry

On of my favourite times of the year.. and now to go spend $24 on six hot cross buns from daily bread 🤣

Amano

Amano Bakery, $21 for 6

Amano are a little cheaper than other premium buns, but just as good. They advertise packs of 6 but put them in random takeaway box. Not so good.

But! I also got one to takeaway. They have a menu for HCB with butter, or jam, or honey, or other things. Which is really nice, but the extra bit, is that they heat the buns with actual flaming flames in a pizza oven type thing. 9/10 currants, sultanas, raisins, orange rind, ginger, flames.

Photo: box got a bit squished on the way home

Simple yet effective

Doe Donuts

Doe Donuts Hot Cross Bun Donut, $9.50

Expensive but really worth it. Cinnamon sugar is good, the cross doesn’t stay on at all, but the dough is perfect texture and spice. Forgot I was eating a donut, but then there was all the sugar and filling. Filling was plain vanilla creme, didn’t need to be anything else.

10/10 yum

Yarrows

Yarrows Cinnamon Jam Donut $6.50 for 4

Sticky and delicious. Jam in the middle with sultanas through the mix. Good for the novelty but biscoff already has the filling bun championship.

7/10 yeah good

I’m a big fan of these ones! I love the cinnamon and jam combo

The Store

The Store, regular, $4ish

Top quality from a cool little place in Britomart. They were sold out on the coffee side but had some on the cafe side. Good mix of spice and fruit. All round good buns.

8/10 could have done with toasting and butter.

Tuck Shop

Tuck Shop, Orange and almond, $10 for 6

Great value for money. I’d heard about Tuck Shop which does Kiwi classic favourites for lunch, like a pie and custard square. Nice and soft with the hot cross buns, they smelt great, tasted good, toasted well, good balance of orange and spice.

9/10 most surprising find.

Kit Kat

KitKat Hot Cross Bun snack size

Good for the novelty, a little generic for “hot cross bun” chocolate. It’s fun, it’s a nice snack, but it’s not a something to rave about this deep into a Facebook thread.

6/10 nice enough

Farro

Farro Chocolate

Quotes from Dee: “Nope” and “it’s like a scavenger hunt to find the chocolate”. They were only slightly improved when toasted.

5/10 no hot cross bun can truly fail but this is the lowest rating this year.

Giapo

Giapo, $22 for 4 Nah. Don’t believe the hype. Lots of fruit and peel, but the texture was wrong, the spice and flavour was wrong. It felt like they weren’t mixed or kneaded or risen well enough. Would they go well with ice cream? I doubt it. 5/10 low score

Nice presentation, but not nice buns
